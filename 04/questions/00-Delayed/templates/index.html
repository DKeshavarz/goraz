I am finally here

I sit in a room full of empty chairs. Each one waits for someone who will never arrive. I speak, and the walls absorb my words without echo. I walk through corridors that lead back to where I started. Outside, the streets are filled with figures I cannot reach—they pass through me like smoke. I knock on doors that are not locked, yet they never open. I write letters to addresses that do not exist. I wait for a hand that will never touch mine. The gray presses in from all sides, and I have forgotten the color of my own face.
